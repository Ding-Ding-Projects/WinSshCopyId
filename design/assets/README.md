# Product mark

`product-mark.svg` is the original project-owned vector master. It combines a public-key symbol with a rightward transfer arrow inside the calm blue rounded-square brand field. It contains no external images, font dependencies, scripts or network references.

Use the mark for the title bar, About view, installer and application icon. The accessible name is the product's current display name; the adjacent product-name label makes the title-bar mark decorative. Do not use this mark as an installation-success indicator or host-trust badge. Status icons are separate semantic controls.

Preserve the square aspect ratio and the full 256-unit canvas. Keep at least 8 units of clear space beyond its outer shape. Use the original blue/white version on both light and dark surfaces, with a suitable surrounding contrast boundary where needed. At small icon sizes retain the key ring, shaft and transfer arrow; do not add tiny lettering. A high-contrast monochrome derivative may replace all foreground strokes with the platform foreground and the background with the platform surface.

Generate native ICO frames from this master at 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 128 and 256 pixels, inspect the small sizes and verify the resulting ICO frame inventory. Raster derivatives and the ICO are not produced by this design lane. No claim of packaged icon verification is made here.
